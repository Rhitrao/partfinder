How it works

Partfinder reads each number and matches it against manufacturers' known numbering formats. That is a match on the shape of the number, not a confirmed identification: no catalogue or document has been checked. Suppliers confirm fitment.

Suppliers are shops Google lists for those brands in your city. Being listed is not a claim that a shop stocks your part.

No prices, stock or lead times are shown: industrial spares are quoted per account, never published.

Nothing you type is stored or logged. Not affiliated with any manufacturer; brand names identify the parts they make.
